Shadow Alibi – Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Shadow Alibi handles information when you use the Shadow Alibi Android game. Please read it together with the privacy disclosures shown in the app and on Google Play.

1. About Shadow Alibi

Shadow Alibi is an Android detective game. Players can play cases, earn experience points and coins, collect stars, maintain season progress, and optionally create or sign in to an account to synchronize game progress using cloud services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information

If you create or use an account, Shadow Alibi uses Firebase Authentication. Depending on the sign-in method you use, this may include your email address and authentication information. If Google Sign-In is enabled for a version of the app, information made available by Google for authentication, such as your name, email address, profile information, and a unique account identifier, may also be processed.

Passwords used with email/password authentication are handled by Firebase Authentication; Shadow Alibi does not have access to your password in readable form.

2.2 Game progress and account data

When you use cloud synchronization, Shadow Alibi may store game information associated with your account, such as:

  • Experience points (XP)
  • Coins
  • Cases solved and completed-case information
  • Stars earned
  • Purchased in-game items
  • Current season progress
  • Previous season records, including XP, cases solved, stars, and highest rank

2.3 Information processed by advertising services

Shadow Alibi uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements, including rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, diagnostics, and other information required for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and delivery, subject to their policies and your applicable consent choices.

During development and testing, Shadow Alibi may use Google's test advertisements. Production advertising configurations may change in future app versions.

2.4 Information stored locally

Shadow Alibi also stores certain gameplay and session information locally on your Android device so that the game can continue to function offline and preserve local progress. Local storage may include game progress, case-rotation information, daily challenge information, and settings needed for gameplay.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Create and authenticate player accounts.
  • Save and restore game progress across sessions and devices when cloud synchronization is used.
  • Maintain XP, coins, stars, case progress, seasons, ranks, and other gameplay records.
  • Provide rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and misuse of advertising or account services.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the game and its supporting services.

4. Third-Party Services

Shadow Alibi uses third-party services that may process information according to their own policies:

  • Firebase Authentication – account creation, sign-in, and authentication.
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore – cloud storage and synchronization of game progress.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) – advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and related services.

These services may process information on our behalf or independently as described in their applicable privacy documentation. Their practices are governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be transmitted to the third-party service providers described above when necessary to provide authentication, cloud synchronization, advertising, security, measurement, or other app functionality.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, security, or integrity of the app and its users.

6. Data Security

Shadow Alibi relies on Firebase and Google services that use security measures appropriate to the services they provide. Data transmitted between the app and supported cloud services is sent using secure network connections where provided by those services.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Data Retention

Account and cloud game data are retained while your account remains active or for as long as necessary to provide the requested services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain legitimate business and security records.

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is removed by the app, cleared by the Android system, or otherwise deleted by you.

8. Account and Data Deletion

If you create an account, you may request deletion of your account and associated Shadow Alibi cloud game data. To request deletion, contact the developer using the privacy contact information provided below and include the email address associated with your account.

After verifying the request as reasonably necessary, the developer will delete the Firebase account and associated cloud game data under the applicable deletion process, except for information that must be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

You can also remove local app data by uninstalling Shadow Alibi or clearing the app's storage through Android settings. Removing local data does not by itself delete an online Firebase account.

9. Children's Privacy

Shadow Alibi is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and deleted when required.

10. Your Choices

  • You can choose whether to create/sign in to an account or continue using guest/local gameplay where available.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated cloud game data.
  • You can manage Android device permissions and applicable advertising/privacy choices provided by Google and the app.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Shadow Alibi's features, third-party services, or data practices change. The updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, data deletion requests, or other privacy-related concerns, contact the Shadow Alibi developer through the contact email shown on the official Google Play listing for Shadow Alibi.
