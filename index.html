1. Information We Collect

Shadow Alibi is designed to minimize personal data collection. The App does not intentionally collect your name, home address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health information, or other personal information directly through the game.

Game Progress and Settings

The App stores gameplay information on your device so that your progress can be retained. This may include items such as:

  • XP and detective rank
  • In-game coin balance
  • Cases solved and completed case numbers
  • Stars earned
  • Daily challenge/streak information
  • Other local game progress and settings required for the App to function

This information is stored locally on your device using Android application storage and is not intentionally transmitted to our own server.
